    What gets pulled out, and how the image links work
    • Block one carries the full criteria table: NAAC, Strategic Plan and Graduate Attribute, each with its number, sub-number and title.
    • Block two carries the event type, all six general information fields, all four speaker fields, participant type and count, highlights, objectives, summary, follow-up plan, rapporteur name and contact, feedback and impact analysis.
    • Bullet lists are kept whole in one cell, with each bullet separated by a dot.
    • Anything in the file that doesn't match a known label is still picked up and added as an extra column at the end, so nothing is dropped.
    • Poster, geo-tagged photos and attendance sheets come out as thumbnails. Save each one, upload it to Drive, then paste its link in the box underneath — the matching column fills in as you paste.
    • When you copy or open in Sheets, a blank column is left between each attribute group — NAAC, Strategic Plan and Graduate Attribute; General Info, Speaker, Participants and so on — so each reads as its own block once pasted. A plain paste can't carry cell borders or shading, so this blank-column gap is what stands in for a boxed table.
    • Open in Google Sheets puts both blocks on your clipboard and opens a blank sheet — click A1 and paste. A page can't write into your Drive without you signing in, so that one paste is the only manual step.
    • In Sheets, merge the two header rows by hand once in your template. After that, everything pastes straight in.
